About This Project

Project Concept

This application preserves the original project concept of animal classification and localization and expands the intended scope to land animals, sea animals and birds.

Classification identifies the model's class; localization marks position using bounding boxes.

Applications

Wildlife monitoring · Conservation · Agriculture · Smart surveillance · Animal tracking · Research & education

Future Scope

Real-time camera detection · Video tracking · Custom species training · Mobile app · Edge deployment
