About Project

Project concept

This application preserves the original animal classification and localization concept and expands its intended scope to land animals, sea animals and birds.

Classification identifies the class supplied by the model; localization identifies object position with a bounding box.

Applications

Wildlife monitoring · Conservation · Agriculture · Smart surveillance · Animal tracking · Research & education.

Future scope

Custom multi-species training · video detection · real-time camera tracking · mobile application · edge deployment.
